# 2026-10-21 — models lane: what the prompt quotes, held out; then the held-outs reviewed and fixed

**Mandate (owner, unchanged):** the biggest free model with an optimal response time; big models first.
**Solved:** yes, for the key-free plan. Every eval message the prompt quotes has a held-out paraphrase that
asks for exactly what its original asks, graded by the same code. A probe keeps it that way, and an
adversarial review's findings are fixed. Every model run still waits on a missing `.env.local`.

## What moved

- **`probe-quotes.mjs`: which eval messages the prompt quotes.**
  - **What it reads:** the prompt the model is sent, date line included. loop-eval's bundle prints it with
    `PRINT_PROMPT`.
  - **What counts as a near-quote**, either test:
    - one prompt line holds at least 75% of a message's content words, and at least three (the week's own
      plan lines do not count);
    - a user example in the prompt holds three or more words of the message, two of them content words,
      verbatim or with one word of the message left out. Day names count as one, curly quotes as straight,
      and an example wrapped over two lines is read whole. The model's side of a worked example (JSON keys and
      values, spaced or not) is no example.
  - **Exit code:** the near-quotes with no held-out, plus any held-out the prompt has come to quote.
  - **Proof it can fail:** `--self-test` plants ten lines in the real prompt and requires the count to move as
    it should (10/10). Each of the six rules added after the review was removed in turn, and the case named
    for it failed every time (6/6).
  - **Before a prompt change lands:** `--code <branch>` or `--prompt-file <draft>`.
  - **On main:** 8 near-quotes in 55 messages, all held out.
  - **First tries that were wrong:**
    - the longest shared run of words missed `contradiction`, which shares only "the whole week" verbatim but
      4 of its 5 content words;
    - reading `promptV2.ts` as text counted code comments the model never sees;
    - the first parser skipped four loop scenarios, and one of them, `find-then-place`, was a quote. A skipped
      scenario now stops the probe.
  - **On the wording branch** it finds exactly the two quotes its lines add, "save some room for it" and
    "have it more often". Both are held out.
- **Five new held-outs (four loop, one conversation), each graded by the same function as its original:**
  - `vague-goal-heldout`: "i want to drop a few pounds, can you help with that?" The original's message IS
    the prompt's CLARIFY example.
  - `emotional-heldout`: "it's that time of the month and i've got zero energy". The original's words are in
    the prompt's list of body states.
  - `every-day-swap-heldout`: "an omelette every morning would make me very happy". The prompt says "pancakes
    every breakfast".
  - `find-then-place-heldout`: "look up a dinner with no meat or fish that's ready in 15 minutes or less and
    stick it on tuesday". The original's requirement is the prompt's own example, "vegetarian, under 20
    minutes". Two dinners `find_recipes` can return qualify; the 18- and 20-minute ones the original allows
    are must-fail controls.
  - convo `vegetarian-then-steak`: "i've gone vegetarian, no more meat for me", then "can i get a steak for
    wednesday dinner?". The original's turn 2 echoes the prompt's vegan/chicken example, so the pair is
    compared on turn 2.
  - The nothing, hollow and garbage fakes score 0 on all five.
- **Both evals print each held-out beside its original:** `held out x/y, their originals x/y`. They name any
  scenario that passed only in the prompt's words, and any held-out whose original was not in the run.
  `fair-arms.mjs` carries the same numbers into its table and manifest.

## The review, and what it changed

An adversarial review ran six reviewers: faithfulness, checks, controls, the probe, the summary and docs, and
scripts that run out. Two finished before the session restarted. The other four left their candidate cases in
the scratch folder; each was re-run through the real loop and judged against the prompt here.

- **Four held-outs reworded**, because each asked for something other than its original:
  - `rate-heldout` said "was a 10/10", handing over the rating the original makes the model infer;
  - `eat-out-heldout` only reported a dinner out, where the original asks for room ("can you leave space for
    that?" now);
  - `days-lighter-heldout` said "the portions", naming the resize the original leaves to judgement ("cut down
    how much i eat" now);
  - `find-then-place-heldout` said "meat-free", which admits fish; the library has seven seafood dinners of 15
    minutes or less.
- **Checks that passed wrong answers, now failing them:**
  - offering to do both halves of a clash ("Want me to make every day vegetarian and put a steak on
    Wednesday's dinner?"). That passed `contradiction`, its held-out and both diet conversations, because
    naming the diet and the dish was enough. The reply must now say they conflict (`namesClash`), and in the
    conversations offer the choices;
  - a correct refusal in the user's own words ("you told me no more meat") failed; `NAMES_VEGETARIAN` accepts it;
  - saving "probably low on iron because of her period" or "heavy periods" as a condition on a feeling;
  - choosing a target for a vague goal without "kcal": "around 1,600 a day", "a 15% cut", "300 fewer calories",
    a "500-calorie" deficit saved to memory (`chosenCalorieFigure`);
  - a protein target cut beside a new diet, in turn 1 of the diet conversations;
  - in find-then-place, a treat `find_recipes` never offers, and an unasked lasting memory ("vegetarian").
- **The scorecards:** the prompt stamp gained `sentSha`, the prompt actually sent (`sha` stays as it was, so
  older scorecards still compare). A held-out naming no original stops the eval at load. fair-arms no longer
  counts an arm that graded nothing as evidence.
- **Rejected:** grading `find-then-place` at "under 20" strictly (a 20-minute dish failing). An earlier review
  already ruled the exact edge acceptable.
- **Sent to v1:** "zero energy" is not among the engine's fatigue triggers, so the held-out's symptom op gets
  the generic note instead of the fatigue offer. And a constrain's cook-time cap has a silent 5-minute grace:
  "15 minutes or less" placed a 20-minute dinner. v1 is taking the cook-time rule.
