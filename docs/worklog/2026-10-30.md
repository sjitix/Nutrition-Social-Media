# 2026-10-30 — D9a hardening: the fuzz sends this week's operations

**Milestone:** V1 D9a (C4), its engine operations under the random-sequence invariants.
**Solved:** in progress. F8, decisions 16 and 17 and D8 still wait on the owner.

## What moved

- **The engine fuzz now sends `move_meal`, `skip_meal`, and one slot under a protein floor or a cook-time limit.**
  None of D9a's operations was in it, so no random sequence had checked them against the invariants: diet,
  allergens, meal count, no repeat in a day, portion bounds, cook time and calories. They are now 17% of the
  operations. 800 sequences, run alone: no violation in the engine. The first runs failed on two faults in the
  test itself:
  - **`calorieReachable` said the opposite of its comment.** For a meal with no base recipe it returned
    "reachable" under the comment "can't judge; don't flag". A skipped lunch is held at 0 kcal, so a day whose
    other two meals were already at the 1.8x ceiling was flagged at 1699 kcal. Such a meal now counts at its own
    calories, as the engine holds it.
  - **A one-slot re-plan that changed nothing ended a day's exemption.** A day made bigger on purpose is
    exempt from the calorie check. "Already has 45g protein" rightly leaves that day as it was, yet the bookkeeping
    cleared the exemption on any `regenerate_day`. Only a change to the day clears it now.
  - `FUZZ_ALL=1` prints every violation rather than the first of each kind.

- **B6, on the ask path: a remembered health condition earns an offer, never a change (D8's parallel item).**
  "I'm pregnant" was remembered, then changed nothing and said nothing about food. The condition-aware selector
  had been built and tested since September but never wired: VISION says ask before adjusting, and the open
  question was how. Now the engine itself writes the offer when a remembered fact makes a nutrient newly called
  for. It names the condition and its nutrients and offers to favour the first one the library can supply under
  the person's diet, with the doctor line. Nothing changes until a yes, which the model sends as
  `constrain(boostNutrient)`. Because the offer is an engine note, it is shown as written, so a model can neither
  skip the question nor claim it already adjusted.
  - No offer for a fact restated, a preference, a denied deficiency ("not deficient in iron"), or a turn that
    already boosts.
  - The prompt now says: remember a condition and change nothing; on a yes, boost.
  - New public names: `conditionOffer` (plan/report) and `liveConditions` (data/conditions). 5 rows.
  - Auto-applying a durable condition to a fresh first week stays out: it needs a place to disclose it, and
    `WeekPlan` has no notes field.
