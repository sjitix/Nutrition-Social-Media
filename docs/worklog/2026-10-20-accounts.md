## What got in the way

- **A green gate that delivered nothing.** The first ship named all 17 files, six of them docs. Its
  gate passed, with `test:engine` at 1010/0, and `ship` exited after 1418 s. But three commits from
  other lanes landed inside that window:
  - `d7e3d20` (models) at 22:50;
  - `0950eb7` (v1's docs) at 22:50;
  - `4667e9e` (models' docs) at 22:51.

  The last two changed `CONTEXT.md` and the worklog index, and `ship` stopped with nothing committed
  (exit 2). It must: merging there, unseen, is how another lane's words get lost (lesson 47). The cost
  was one 24-minute gate, with v1's next commit waiting behind it. **The fix is the split:** code
  gated, shared docs with `--no-gate`, which `ship` allows because no gate reads a doc. Lesson 69.
- **An adjacent-rows conflict in the worklog index.** v1 and the models lane had each added a
  2026-10-20 row, one either side of this lane's 2026-10-18 row. I checked that their copy of this
  lane's row still equalled the base, then took their file and swapped in this lane's version of that
  one row.
