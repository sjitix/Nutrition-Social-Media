## What moved

- **F4's own adversarial review, before it shipped** (workflow `wf_cbcccd36-161`: four lenses, then a skeptic per
  serious finding, 16 agents, 55 minutes). It found 31 issues: 11 major, 16 minor, 4 nit. All 12 that went to a
  skeptic reproduced and none was refuted. Five of the majors were F4's own:
  - **The covered rule ignored the matcher's exceptions.** Under a stored "gluten", "I'm allergic to tortillas"
    was called already covered and stored nothing, so 11 dishes with corn tortillas stayed plannable and a later
    swap placed fajitas (LC-23). `exclusionCovers` now judges every exception phrase too.
  - **"I have a glass noodle allergy" stored "noodle"**, whose own exceptions serve glass noodles (LC-24). Splitting a
    phrase went back to the narrow food test, and a phrase whose words do not block it is kept whole.
  - **The confirm-before-act tail excused a promise when it asked about a different change** ("Should I do that for
    Wednesday too?"), and a bare "OK?" excused one while another sentence said the change was done.
  - **The strict scope refused turns the parent ran** (`{slot: null}`, `"all"`, `mealType`), losing an allergy sent in
    the same turn. A scope is now read the way the schema reads it before validation (`providers/turnShape.ts`).
  - **An import titled like a library dish lost the library twin's check**, which had caught its flour by accident.
    Its own text was being misread (the next item).
- **Two allergen holes older than F4, fixed in it:**
  - **A dish's parts ran together for the matcher** (LC-25, since C). Joined with spaces, "cocoa" then "butter" read as
    cocoa butter and "rice" then "flour" as rice flour, so butter passed a dairy allergy and flour a wheat one.
    `mealHaystack` keeps the parts apart for every matcher call on a dish: over the library it changes 0 of
    198,396 recipe/word verdicts.
  - **A tortilla, noodle or cracker allergy inherited the gluten words' exceptions** (LC-26): "I have a tortilla
    allergy" served corn tortillas. Those three now keep blocking what gluten excepts. Flour is the owner's call
    (decision 9, below).
- **The rest of the review's findings that F4 could fix, fixed:** a boost's lines said "about 35mg of iron a day" over
  a week of 21.9 mg after a switch to meal-prep. What meal-prep cannot apply is now said once, judged on the mode the
  call ends in. An unset mode reads as fresh. "Your allergies haven't changed" is taken back on the agent loop's path
  too, judged by what each list blocks. Import notes say the allergy in the person's words, never the stored sentence.
  "Made the other meals lighter" is said only when they got lighter. A sentence ends inside a closing quote or bold.
- **Re-measuring after each fix found three over-blocks the fixes made** (lesson 72). Each was caught by re-running the
  stored-allergy comparison against the parent:
  - the matcher's kept compounds, split, stored "corn", "rice" and "zucchini";
  - a sentence the model labelled an allergy ("prefers a light dinner after a big lunch") was stored whole;
  - "tree nut" was stored as "nuts".
  Final figures against the parent:
  - 0 of 126 stored `remember` facts bind differently;
  - under three stored allergies, 1,800 rows: 237 block more, 124 change only the note, 0 block less;
  - 0 of 145 stored scopes are refused or changed;
  - over 1,801 stored replies, 2 claim verdicts change, both intended.
- **Proof:** the whole new suite bundled and run against the parent `ef71993`, 1,167 rows: 1,117 pass, 50 fail. Of 66
  new rows, the 45 that test a change fail there and the 21 guards pass. The other 5 failures are rows reworded for

- **Decision 9 gets a fourth row (flour).** A bare "flour" allergy blocks 1 of 501 recipes and lets 5 through (chickpea
  flour 3, oat flour 2), because flour's exceptions are the gluten-free flours. The recommendation is to block every

## What got in the way

- **A covered-rule fix that measured fine on the facts the models produce was wrong on the words people type.** The
  first comparison ran over the 126 stored facts and found nothing. The review's probe ran every pair of a stored
  category and an allergen word (6,201 covered pairs, 361 unsound). The corpus had never held "tortillas" under
  "gluten". Lesson 71 again, and the reason the comparison now runs over the vocabulary as well as the corpus.
- **Widening a test to fix one hole opened another three times** (lesson 72): the wide food test inside a phrase,
  the no-cue phrase path, and the plural stems. Each was found only because the measurement was re-run after the fix
  rather than trusted from before it.
- **The boost's upgrade loop was tried for D7's records and changed nothing** (0 of 9 runs), so it was reverted, not
  shipped.
- **F4's first gate went red on a flaky row from F1** ("loop undo: [swap Mon], then [undo, swap Tue]"), and nothing was
  committed. The agent-loop section draws an unseeded week, and the row picked its Tuesday dish from three names
  minus three it ruled out. In 5 of 60 seeded weeks none was left, on main as on F4, so the swap did nothing. With
  five names, 0 of 200 weeks fail. It cost a gate run, and the accounts lane waited on the engine slot for it
  (lesson 73).
