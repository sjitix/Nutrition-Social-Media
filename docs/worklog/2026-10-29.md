## What moved

- **One meal under a constraint (`fc01cdf`).** "Only 20 minutes for tonight's dinner" changes that dinner alone.
  A slot-scoped constrain used to be answered "I can't change just your dinner that way yet". It now maps to
  `regenerate_day` with a `mealType`: that slot gets the dish the selector picks under the constraint, and the rest
  of the day stays, with portions balanced. A per-meal TARGET ("more protein at breakfast") still has no engine path,
  and still says so. 4 rows, 2 failing on the parent.
  - **The first gate run caught an older row:** it pinned "nothing is re-planned" for a
    `{slot: lunch, days: [Wednesday]}` vegetarian constrain, which was the limitation this removes. That row now checks
    the new behaviour: Wednesday's lunch alone becomes vegetarian, and every other meal keeps its dish.
- **Skip a meal (`1b658d1`, then the `skip` primitive).** "I'm skipping lunch today": the slot becomes a 0 kcal record
  (`record: "skipped"`), the rest of the day is re-solved to carry it, and every re-plan keeps it, as a logged meal
  is kept. A logged meal, a meal out, a pin or an already-skipped slot is refused with its reason. A skipped snack
  whose slot goes (4 meals a day to 3) leaves quietly. The meal sheet has "Skip this meal", Week labels it
  "Skipped", Today counts it at 0 kcal, and the palette takes "skip lunch today" and "move tue dinner to thu". The
  chat primitive `{op:"skip", day, slot}` followed (`51036bd`), after the models lane was told (`3d67357`). 6 engine rows, 4
  palette rows.
- **22 recipes, 501 to 523 (`ab3a454`): no cell under two weeks of variety.** Measured first: keto lunch 12
  and dinner 11, and quick dinners (20 minutes or less) 4 for vegans and 11 for keto, were the only cells under 14.
  4 keto lunches, 8 keto dinners and 10 quick vegan dinners, from curated ingredients only. Each was checked before it
  went in (keto under 20 g carbs, the slot's calorie floor, 4/4/9 within 16%, coverage, diet tags).
  `check:ingredients` then caught two display names that resolved only through their slug. The first gate caught two
  more things: the tacos' method said "tortillas", which the gluten matcher reads as wheat; and a cook-time row assumed
  no dinner takes 10 minutes or less. That row now takes its limit from the library. After: keto 16/19, quick vegan
  dinners 14, quick keto dinners 14.
- **19 more recipes, 523 to 542 (`fe65646`): variety inside the cells.** Keto lunches had been 15 of 16 American or
  Mediterranean, and keto dinners had no Indian dish. Each keto cell now has every cuisine, and vegan lunches and
  breakfasts have more than one Italian dish. `check:drafts` caught a dinner under its floor, a "Tortilla" in a
  gluten_free dish's own name, and a gluten_free tag on turkey sausage. The gate then failed one row that had pinned
  "spinach (cooked)" at 50 dishes, while the library now has 52. That row checks for a leaking qualifier, so it now
  compares each qualified dislike with its plain food (all five equal).
- **11 high-protein vegan dishes, 542 to 553 (`3244ed1`).** Targets are ~150 g of protein a day, and only 4 of 33
  vegan breakfasts reached 25 g. Now 11 of 40; vegan lunches 18 to 22. **Its first gate failed 8 rows, and two were
  real defects the new dishes exposed:**
  - **"Italian, and more iron" kept 4 Italian dishes** (Italian alone: 18–20), while the reply said "…while making
    the other changes you asked for". The boost's upgrade took the densest dish in the library for each slot, and the
    new tofu, tempeh and edamame dishes are iron-dense and not Italian. Now a slot already holding the asked cuisine
    is upgraded only within it: 11–12 Italian dishes.
  - **A swap could go unmentioned.** A one-day swap's note was its figures alone. When a later step in the same reply
    changed the day, those stale figures were dropped, and with them the only trace of the swap. Its note now starts
    "Swapped Monday's dinner to …".
  The other six depended on what seeded weeks happened to contain, and now say what they mean: a fixture placed
  rather than searched for, more seeds, a target that ensures a shortfall, and the dinners a swap placed rather than
  the whole week. One more was a real rounding edge, the wrap's calories summing to 376.4999… one way and 376.5 the
  other.
- **8 inexpensive keto dinners, 553 to 561 (`3616273`).** A low budget allows cost 2. Under it keto had 14 dinners,
  on the two-week line, and none at cost 1. Eggs, chicken thighs, turkey and canned tuna: 22 dinners at cost 2 or
  less, 7 at cost 1.
- **19 dinners of 15 minutes or less, 561 to 580 (`b2cf31b`).** With `maxCookTime` 15 a vegan had 6 dinners, a
  Mediterranean eater 6, keto 7, vegetarian 10 and gluten-free 12. Now 14, 16, 14, 19 and 22. All 19 passed
  `check:drafts` on its first run.
- **8 high-protein Mediterranean breakfasts, 580 to 588 (`4803483`).** 19 of 35 had reached 25 g and only 4
  reached 30 g. Now 27 and 7.
- **`propose:ingredient` (B7).** `npm run propose:ingredient -- "smoked tofu"` ranks USDA candidates for a new
  ingredient and shows, for each, its per-100 g kcal, protein, carbs and fat. It also checks whether 4/4/9 adds up,
  whether a curated slug already uses that food, and whether the food is the same one by name. On its first run the
  top candidates were "Tofu yogurt" for smoked tofu and "Salmonberries" for salmon fillet, so it prints an entry
  template to fill from the chosen row, never a pick. It writes nothing.
  - Also fixed: `usda-search.mjs` ran its own search on whatever arguments its importer was given.
- **`check:drafts`.** Draft recipes are checked as the library will check them before they go into `seeds.ts`:
  curated names, macros, floors, keto, 4/4/9, coverage, tags, and a gluten allergy reading the method. The batch of 22

- **Decision 16 widened.** The palette's "protein 180" and the per-day protein control use the same portion balancer,
