## What moved

- **F5a-1 landed** (`950d243`).
  - An urgent-care reply, an explanation, or any answer the engine gave survives a later model failure. The
    route's 503 "offline" now needs nothing changed AND nothing said. A crisis outranks an urgent reply, and a
    crisis ends the run, as the pre-scan's does.
  - A turn the model sent but nobody could read keeps its remember and symptom ops, read leniently, on every
    unreadable turn. So a stated allergy is no longer lost with a malformed op. The model gets one nudge-and-retry.
  - Text that is not one JSON object is scanned for a turn, or at least its ops. A blind retry that brings only
    words is still the failure, so "Noted." never stands over an unsaved allergy.
  - The provider says why a call failed (`ModelFailure`): an empty answer is the host's failure, and Claude's parse
    failures read as unreadable. The route logs the kind.
  - 19 rows: 16 fail on the parent, showing the old losses (the urgent run ending in a 503; the refused turn leaving
    no allergy and the peanut bowl).
- **F5a-1's review changed it before it shipped** (three lenses, 10 majors). The skeptics were lost to the session
  restart, so I checked every major with a probe before fixing it. The first version:
  - ended the run on ANY urgent reply, which cut an allergy the model's next step would have bound, and let an urgent
    reply outrank a later crisis;
  - salvaged only strict-parsed remembers (a label "Allergy" or null lost the allergy), dropped the symptom ops that
    carry the urgent reply, and salvaged only within the retry (not on the cap step);
  - nudged on an empty answer, turning a host failure into the model's;
  - lost a cut-off answer entirely;
  - filed the Claude SDK's parse failures as unreachable.
- **F6, the crisis op, landed** (`8c2519a`, 1203/0).
  - `{op:"crisis"}`: the reviewed crisis reply, nothing else of the turn applied, the run ends, no log line.
  - The prompt's two lines, verbatim.
  - `redFlag` reads "do not" as "don't" and "wanna" as "want to". That moves none of the 37 listed phrasings and
    catches the four respellings that slipped.
  - 5 rows, all failing on main.
  - **Measured by the models lane before it landed** (Nemotron-3-Ultra 550B, arms N and convoN, 3 repeats, `803ac71`):
    12 of 12 crisis lines against 6 of 12 on main. Both held-out crisis phrasings went from F F F to P P P. All 15

- **The history-allergy design, agreed with the models lane.**
  - Allergies stated in earlier messages are bound before the first model call: each cue's own clause, through the
    same remember gate the live path uses.
  - Hypotheticals and later denials are skipped.

    lane's catch). The accounts lane adds its shape check.
- **Measured on the live remember gate:** fed a whole sentence, it binds the foods asked for ("…add more chicken and
  rice?" binds chicken and rice) and a hypothetical ("what if i was allergic to eggs?"). The model normally stores
  only the fact, but it is why the history scan cuts clauses.
