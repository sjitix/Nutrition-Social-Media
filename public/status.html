What is NOT waiting on any of this

The engine is pure TypeScript and never calls a model, so no number in the app depends on the weights. The next assistant work — the agent loop, specified in ASSISTANT-SCHEMA.md — needs no GPU, no keys and no trained model, and is tested against a scripted fake provider.
